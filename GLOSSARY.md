# Car Game

The language of this open-world street racing game.

## Language

**Free drive**:
Driving around the city without a prescribed race route or race objective.

**City grid**:
The network of city streets and intersections available for driving and exploration.

**Chase camera**:
A third-person view that follows the player's car and keeps the road ahead visible.

**Controlled slide**:
A recoverable loss of tire grip that the driver can initiate with throttle or handbrake.

**Fleet**:
The selectable set of prepared cars available in free drive.

**Car edition**:
A game-owned copy of a source model with recorded material and assembly choices, wheel pivots and chassis dimensions.

**Baseline car**:
The original GT4 Nissan 350Z edition used to compare fleet integration and driving checks.
